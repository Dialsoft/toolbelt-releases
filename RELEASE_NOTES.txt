TOOL BELT - RELEASE NOTES
=========================

Version 1.2.0  (November 9, 2026)
---------------------------------
* New installer: ToolBeltSetup.exe. It installs Tool Belt just for you
  (no administrator password) and lets you choose:
  - Start Tool Belt when Windows starts (on by default)
  - a Start menu shortcut (on) and a Desktop shortcut (off)
  Hover over any option in Setup to see what it does.
* Tool Belt now appears in Settings > Apps > Installed apps, with an
  uninstaller. Uninstalling asks whether to keep your slots and settings.
* Setup closes a running Tool Belt by itself, so installing over an
  older copy just works. Your slots and settings are kept.
* Installed copies still update themselves automatically, and the
  version shown in Installed apps stays correct after each update.
* The portable ToolBelt.exe (no installer) is still available too.

Version 1.1.0  (November 9, 2026)
---------------------------------
* Automatic updates. Tool Belt checks dialsoft.com for a new version
  15 seconds after it starts and every 6 hours after that.
  - When one is ready, a window shows what's new and offers
    "Update now", "Remind me later" or "Skip this version".
    Nothing installs unless you click "Update now".
  - Every download is checked three ways before it's used: exact size,
    SHA-256 fingerprint, and the version stamped inside the program.
    If any check fails, your current copy is left untouched.
  - The belt disappears for a moment and comes back on the new version.
    Your slots and settings are kept.
  - If you click "Remind me later", an orange dot on the belt button
    reminds you, and "Install update" appears in the belt menu.
* New belt menu items: "Check for updates now", "Check for updates
  automatically" (on by default, takes effect immediately) and
  "Release notes".
* After an update, Tool Belt shows these release notes automatically.
* The user guide has a new "Updates" section.

Version 1.0.0  (November 9, 2026)
---------------------------------
* First release.
* Docks across the top of the screen like a second taskbar. It can't
  be moved, and maximized windows stop just below it.
* 12 slots for programs, shortcuts, documents, folders or websites.
  Fill a slot by dragging something onto it, or click an empty slot to
  browse.
* Right-click a slot to open it, run it as administrator, rename it,
  add command-line options, open its file location, move it or clear it.
  Drag one slot onto another to swap them.
* Belt menu: Small / Medium / Large, names on or off, center the slots,
  clock, choose the monitor, start with Windows. All changes are instant.
* Steps behind full-screen games and videos, then comes back on top.
* Re-docks by itself when monitors or the resolution change, or when
  Windows Explorer restarts.
* Detailed tooltips on everything, plus a built-in user guide.
